Methodology

Dual-Vector True SG — Separates All Courses baseline (SG Base) from Top-20 Similar Courses performance (SG Sim) with sample-weighted regression. Delta Fit measures TPC Twin Cities-specific uplift vs. general skill. Decay weights: Base stability-heavy [6m:20%, 12m:30%, 24m:50%]; Delta recency-heavy [6m:50%, 12m:30%, 24m:20%]. Clamped to ±0.50 SG/round. VTS z-scored to mean=50, std=15.

Glossary — 3M Open 2026

Score Components
VTS 0–100
Venue Tier Score: z-scored composite of SG Similar Composite across the full field (mean=50, std=15)
NSI 0–100
Neutral Skill Index: z-scored SG Base Composite — general skill ranking independent of venue fit
SG Base
Baseline skill across all courses — stability-weighted composite (6m:20%, 12m:30%, 24m:50%)
SG Sim
Similar-course strokes gained — sample-weighted regression using Top-20 courses most similar to TPC Twin Cities
Δ Fit
Delta between SG Sim and SG Base — TPC Twin Cities venue fit signal; positive = course specialist upside; clamped ±0.50

Cut%
