VenueDNA Glossary

Ranking
VenueDNA Rank
The model's pre-tournament ranking of a player's projected structural fit at this venue. Rank 1 represents the strongest projected fit. Rank does not incorporate live scoring, player odds, or market prices.
Tier
A tier-group derived from rank and model confidence. Tier boundaries are determined per-event by the VenueDNA engine — approximate field sizes are listed below.
  • T1 — Elite venue fit (ranks ~1–5, this field). High-conviction projection; full player brief available.
  • T2 — Strong venue fit (ranks ~6–12, this field). Solid structural case; full player brief available.
  • T3 — Moderate venue fit (ranks ~13–25, this field). Case has depth but limited structural edge.
  • T4 — Below-field-average fit (ranks ~26–40, this field). Structural risks identified.
  • T5 — Weak or thin-evidence fit (remaining field). Low conviction; zero-fill or thin evidence data common.
VTS — Venue Trait Score
A 0–100 composite score representing how well a player's tracked skills align with this venue's scoring demands. VTS is not a win probability, predicted finish, or betting-market price. A higher VTS indicates stronger projected structural fit at this venue, before application of gates and penalties.
Player Card Content
Structural Case
The primary venue-trait mechanism driving this player's ranking — the specific skill or statistical edge that makes this venue a structurally strong (or weak) fit. Drawn from the full player brief for T1/T2 players; sourced from the canonical event payload for other tiers with a "limited player brief" label.
Risk / Failure Condition
The specific scenario in which the structural case collapses — the trait or pattern that, if it regresses toward field average, removes the projected edge. This is distinct from the general Risks section: it names the model's key fragility, not a relative weakness list.
Trait Scores
Trait Score (0–100)
A per-skill component score on a 0–100 scale. Higher values indicate stronger alignment on that individual scored trait. Trait scores are pre-composite inputs to the VTS model — they are not VTS itself, and should not be treated as field percentiles. Tier and rank reflect the full model output including weighting, penalties, and gates applied to the composite.
Trait Availability
The evidence state for each individual trait score:
  • Measured — Directly observed from a source data file for this player.
  • Derived — Computed or modeled from measured inputs; not directly observed.
  • Unavailable — Source data absent; score was zero-filled. Not eligible for badges or narrative-trait use.
Badges and Flags
Playstyle Badge
A skill label earned when a player scores above the 80th field percentile on a badge's required trait(s) — for example, "Iron Surgeon" requires top-quintile approach and iron-play scores. Badges are qualified upstream by the VenueDNA engine using computed field percentiles from source data; they are not inferred in the browser from the frozen event artifact. For this event build, badge qualification was not emitted — badge filters are therefore disabled.
Anti-Pattern Flag
A structural risk flag applied by the engine when a player's known weaknesses directly conflict with this venue's primary scoring demands. Anti-pattern flags appear in the Risks section of the player card (and as a ⚠ indicator in the roster). They are not playstyle badges and do not affect tier or rank assignment directly — they document a model-level concern about structural fit.
Benchmark
Benchmark Disagreement
A read-only external comparison between VenueDNA rank and the CH Model benchmark rank. A disagreement flags where the two systems rank a player meaningfully differently. This is an informational audit signal only — it does not alter VenueDNA scoring, tiers, probabilities, or rank. Benchmark data is an external reference and is explicitly not part of the VenueDNA model output.
