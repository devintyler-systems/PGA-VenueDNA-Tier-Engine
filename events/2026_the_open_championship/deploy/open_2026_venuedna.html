Scores & Metrics
VTS — Venue Tier Score
Composite ranking metric: NSI 40% + VFS 30% + VHN 15% + Form 15%. Scale 0–100. Primary sort key.
NSI — Neutral Skill Index
Baseline skill from strokes-gained and shot-quality metrics, normalised across the field. Venue-agnostic. Scale 0–100.
VFS — Venue Fit Score
Alignment between player skill shape and Birkdale's structural demands. Rewards iron-dominant, OTT-accurate profiles. GSO form 1.25× multiplier applied. Scale 0–100.
VHD — Venue History Delta
Adjustment from actual Royal Birkdale SG history. Positive = historically outperforms model. Depth-gated: <6 Birkdale rounds triggers R4 penalty on excess VHN above 50.
Tier Thresholds
T1 ≥78.0 · T2 ≥63.5 · T3 ≥48.0 · T4 ≥33.0 · T5 <33.0. Engine thresholds 2 pts below spec §9 defaults.
Links Weights (Birkdale)
OTT Accuracy 16% (+4% vs default), OTT Positional 18% (−2%), App Overall 13% (−2%). Precision par 70 layout penalises length-without-accuracy profiles.

Projected Scoring (Modal)
Win% / Top 5–20% / Cut%
Model-estimated finish probabilities from VTS via field-calibrated logistic curves. Updated live with SG data during rounds.
WCS — Win Ceiling Score
Maximum potential output given optimal conditions. High WCS + high NSI = ceiling play candidate.
CS — Contention Score
Expected contention band — where the model projects the player finishes on average across field simulations.
FS — Floor Score
Downside output protection. Low FS = high variance / cut-risk player. Adjusted by wave penalty during live rounds.
CSS — Cut Survival Score
Composite cut likelihood combining FS, NSI, and historical cut rate at links venues. Above 60 = reliable cut maker.
Scoring Band
Expected range label for the player's projected output (e.g., "Contender Band", "Dark Horse Window"). Derived from tier + penalty profile.
rho — Spearman Correlation
Live model-to-field rank correlation. Values >0.35 = strong predictiveness. Displayed on live round leaderboard header.
Best Betting Lane
R1 — PUTT Regression
High-NSI players propped by non-transferable putting. Without links T2G confirmation: −4.0 VTS (no links) / −2.0 (borderline).
R2 — Zero Links
No links/Birkdale rounds — modelled delta unconstrained. Cap: ±1.5, excess removed from VTS.
R3 — OTT-Only Links
Positive GSO OTT but collapsed APP. Birkdale precision layout punishes length-without-accuracy: −5.0 VTS.
R4 — Depth Gate
Thin Birkdale sample (<6 rounds) inflates VHN. Penalty: −0.15 × excess VHN above 50.
R6 — Putt-Led Total
Positive GSO driven by putting alone. Wind-exposed Birkdale greens don't transfer: −3.0 VTS.
R7 — Links APP Gate
Below-threshold approach at links. Birkdale demands elite iron play inside 175 yds: −2.5 VTS / −1.5 (borderline).
R9 — Form Spike
