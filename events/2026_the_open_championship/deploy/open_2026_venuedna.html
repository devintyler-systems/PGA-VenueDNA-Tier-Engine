Engine & Methodology

VenueDNA Tier Engine v3 + Audit Corrections · 9 rules
NSI — Neutral Skill Index

Baseline skill score from strokes-gained and shot-quality metrics, normalised across the field. Reflects ball-striking, approach, and putting competency independent of venue. Scale 0–100.

VFS — Venue Fit Score

Alignment between a player's skill shape and Birkdale's structural demands. Rewards iron-dominant profiles, OTT accuracy, approach precision. Boosted by confirmed GSO links form (1.25× multiplier). Scale 0–100.

VHD — Venue History Delta

Adjustment from actual Royal Birkdale SG history (all-time rounds). Positive = historically outperforms model. Depth-gated: fewer than 6 Birkdale rounds triggers R4 penalty on excess VHN above 50.

Audit Penalties (R1–R9)

Nine structural risk rules subtracting VTS when a player's profile is exposed at links. Penalties range from −0.15×excess (R4 depth gate) to −5.0 (R3 OTT-only, R6 putt-led) to −9.1 (compound R3+R7).
